Privacy Policy

D-sales Pro uses Google OAuth to access only the data you explicitly permit. Leads and spreadsheet data remain in your Google account.

Data we access

  • Google Sheets read/write access (only when you connect a sheet)
  • Google Drive file listing (only to let you pick a spreadsheet)

How to remove access

Visit your Google Account > Security > Third-party apps with account access and remove D-sales Pro.
